## 📌 Project Summary

This project analyzes hospital patient data to understand **patient readmissions, bed occupancy, and resource utilization**.

The dashboard provides actionable insights to help hospitals **optimize bed management, reduce readmissions, and improve operational efficiency**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Hospitals often struggle with:

* High readmission rates
* Inefficient bed utilization
* Long patient stays reducing bed turnover
* Seasonal spikes in admissions
* Poor follow-up care for certain patient groups

This project addresses these issues using **data-driven insights and visualization**.

## 📈 Key Insights

### 🧓 High-Risk Population

* Average Age: **61 years**
* Risk Score: **0.84**
  ➡️ Elderly patients require longer care, increasing bed occupancy

---

### 🛏️ Bed Utilization Drivers

* Patients with **multiple comorbidities** stay longer (6–10 days)
* Chronic diseases lead to repeated admissions
  ➡️ High bed occupancy and reduced turnover

---

### 🌍 Regional Demand Imbalance

* South region has highest patient load (~24.7%)
  ➡️ Resource strain and uneven bed availability

---

### 💊 Chronic Diseases Impact

* Diabetes, Hypertension, COPD are leading diagnoses
  ➡️ Continuous hospital admissions affecting capacity

---

### 🏥 Length of Stay Bottlenecks

* Stroke (10 days) and COPD (9 days)
  ➡️ Major contributors to bed blocking

---

### ❄️ Seasonal Bed Pressure

* Peak admissions in **December & January**
  ➡️ Increased infections and comorbidities
  ➡️ Higher bed occupancy during winter

---

### 💳 Insurance Gap

* Lower follow-up among uninsured patients
  ➡️ Higher readmission → increased bed usage

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### 🛏️ Bed Management Optimization

* Improve discharge planning to reduce length of stay
* Increase bed capacity during peak seasons
* Prioritize high-risk patient monitoring

---

### 🔄 Reduce Readmissions

* Strengthen follow-up systems
* Focus on chronic disease management
* Improve patient education and preventive care

---

### 📊 Resource Planning

* Allocate more beds and staff to high-demand regions
* Use historical data for demand forecasting
* Optimize staff scheduling based on admission trends

---

### ❄️ Seasonal Preparedness

* Increase capacity during winter months
* Plan for infection-related case surges
* Ensure availability of critical care units
